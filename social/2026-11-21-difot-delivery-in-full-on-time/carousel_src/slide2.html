How to calculate it.

DIFOT % = LINES IN FULL AND ON TIME ÷ LINES DUE × 100
1List

Every order line due in the period, including lines not yet delivered

2Test

Full quantity and the agreed date. A line must pass both at once

3Divide

Passing lines over lines due, times 100

4Don't multiply

On-time rate × in-full rate counts late-and-short lines twice

5Find causes

One cause per failed line, from a short fixed list

6Publish

The percentage, with the failed lines beside it, reviewed weekly
